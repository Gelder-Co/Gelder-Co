WEB3 & DIGITAL ASSETS

Curiosity is fine. Confusion is expensive.

What it is

Staking & on-chain yield

Returns from network rewards or trading fees. High-APY cases often use new depositor funds — not sustainable.

Ask first

Custody & contracts

Who holds the keys? Is it audited? Can you exit instantly? If you can't answer these, you don't understand the product yet.

Red flag

"Guaranteed" returns

No legitimate on-chain product guarantees a fixed return. "Guaranteed X% monthly" is the most reliable scam indicator.

Our view

Position sizing > prediction

Never put in more than you can lose without it changing your life. Treat every projection as a guess with a number.
