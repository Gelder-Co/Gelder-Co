QUESTIONS

Quick answers.

What is Gelder & Co.?+

Gelder & Co. provides educational tools, worksheets, and systems to help you understand your financial position and build better habits. We're not a licensed advisor — we help you think more clearly about money so you can make informed decisions.

What is the Money Scorecard?+

A 15-question interactive assessment that evaluates your financial foundation across 6 key areas: Cash Flow, Debt & Credit, Wealth Building, Risk Management, Financial Knowledge, and Opportunity Readiness. Takes about 2 minutes. Completely free and private.

What do I get with the Operating Kit?+

The Money Scorecard, personal finance dashboards, monthly cash flow planner, debt strategy planner, savings goal planner, business finance tools, AI workflow library, and quarterly planning guidance. Everything you need in one system.

Is this financial advice?+

No. These are educational guides and tools. We don't know your full situation, so we can't give personalized recommendations. Speak with a licensed advisor for advice tailored to you.

Do I need financial experience?+

No. Everything is designed for people who are just getting started. We explain concepts clearly, provide templates and worksheets, and break things down step-by-step.

Can I use this for my business?+

Yes. The Operating Kit and Premium Systems include business finance tools, cash flow dashboards for businesses, and planning templates designed for both personal and business money management.

Can I cancel anytime?+

Yes. Cancel anytime from your account. For monthly and quarterly plans, you lose access at the end of your billing period. For annual and lifetime, you keep everything you've downloaded.
